# The BDOS's system functions through `$F37D`

> **Spec:** FINAL (2026-11-22), the core rules: review pass 2's findings were answered by round 3 with no
> new behaviour (the weekday, refusals writing no field, the month length, the accepted edges, DSKRST
> whatever E) and the rest are combinations, now [A] (docs/spec-method.md §7, the small-call stop). Three
> measured rounds on two references; scratchpad/BSYS_review1.md, BSYS_review2.md.
> One page for eleven small functions sharing one probe: VERSION ($0C), DSKRST ($0D), SELDSK ($0E),
> LOGIN ($18), CURDRV ($19), SETDTA ($1A), ALLOC ($1B), GDATE / SDATE ($2A / $2B), GTIME / STIME
> ($2C / $2D).

## Summary

The disk ROM puts a BDOS entry at $F37D: C the function, the rest as MSX-DOS takes them. Disk BASIC
and ROM programs call it without MSX-DOS loaded. Through it:

- **VERSION** answers $22 (CP/M 2.2) in A and HL.
- **CURDRV** answers drive A (0), **SELDSK** the number of drives (2: drive A and its phantom B),
  **LOGIN** the online-drive bitmap (3).
- **SETDTA** sets the DTA cell ($F23D) to DE; it is $0000 until then, and **DSKRST** resets it to
  $0080 and makes drive A current again, whatever E holds.
- **ALLOC** answers the disk's sectors per cluster (A), sector size (BC), clusters (DE) and free
  clusters (HL), with IX pointing AT the drive's DPB and IY at the FAT buffer; E 1 is drive A, as 0.
- **GDATE** reads the date, 1984-01-01 after start-up, with the day of the week in A (Sunday 0);
  **SDATE** sets a valid date (A 0; 01-01 and 12-31 included) and refuses months 0 and 13, day 0 and a
  day past the month's own length (32, November 31) with A $FF, writing no field of the stored date. **GTIME** reads 0:00:00 -- an MSX1 has no clock -- and stays 0 after **STIME**,
  which takes a valid time (A 0; 23:59:59 included) and refuses hours 24 and 25, minute 60 and
  second 60 (A $FF).
- Interrupts are off after every call.

zerobas answers VERSION, CURDRV, SELDSK, LOGIN, ALLOC's numbers, GDATE, GTIME and DSKRST's drive the
same, and SDATE / STIME too (taking a valid date / time and refusing the rest, since D-F37DSETDATE,
2026-11-22: they refused every one). Through $F37D it leaves interrupts on
(D-F37DEI), leaves IX / IY alone after ALLOC (D-ALLOCIXIY), starts the DTA cell at $0080 and does not
reset it on DSKRST (D-F37DDTA), and returns other working registers (D-F37DREGS).

## The specification

References: the Philips VG-8020 with an NMS 1200 disk interface (row `D`) AND the National CF-3300
(row `C`); every rule holds on both. Ours: zerobas's disk build.

**Evidence.** **[XS rNN]**: case `NN` on row `r` of the XBSYS1 probe -- run 1 for row D
([`scratchpad/XBSYS1_run1.out`](../../scratchpad/XBSYS1_run1.out), `--row disk`), run 2 for row C
([`scratchpad/XBSYS1_run2.out`](../../scratchpad/XBSYS1_run2.out), `--row cf3300`).
**[XS2 rNN]**: XBSYS2 the same way, runs 1 (row D) and 2 (row C)
([`XBSYS2_run1.out`](../../scratchpad/XBSYS2_run1.out), [`XBSYS2_run2.out`](../../scratchpad/XBSYS2_run2.out)):
SDATE / GDATE of a Monday, a refused SDATE then GDATE, the SDATE / STIME edges, ALLOC with E 0 and 1 (the
record adds the 4 bytes at IX and at IY after ALLOC), SELDSK 1, CURDRV, DSKRST, CURDRV, LOGIN
(`probes/bios/spec/xbsys2.asm`). **[XS3 rNN]**: XBSYS3, runs 1 (row D) and 2 (row C)
([`XBSYS3_run1.out`](../../scratchpad/XBSYS3_run1.out), [`XBSYS3_run2.out`](../../scratchpad/XBSYS3_run2.out)):
a Tuesday, refused SDATEs each followed by GDATE (month 13 of 1999, January 32, November 31), the
accepted edges, 23:59:59, and DSKRST with E 1 (`probes/bios/spec/xbsys3.asm`).
`probes/bios/spec/xbsys1.asm` documents the frame (XDENT1's H.STKE cartridge, so the disk ROM is up but
neither BASIC nor MSX-DOS runs; drive A the generated empty 720 KB disk), the 15 cases in order (VERSION,
CURDRV, SELDSK, LOGIN, SETDTA $A800, ALLOC, GDATE, SDATE 2026-11-22, GDATE, GTIME, STIME 12:34:56, GTIME,
DSKRST, SDATE month 13, STIME hour 25) and the 15-byte record: A, F, B, C, D, E, H, L, the interrupt
state, IX, IY, the DTA cell. Every case enters with IX $1111, IY $2222, interrupts off.

## On later generations (MSX2, MSX2+)

Measured 2026-11-23 on the HB-F900 and NMS 8250 (MSX2) and the FS-A1WSX (MSX2+), each with its own built-in drive and disk ROM: the page's probes through `probes/bios/biosl_run.py --row msx2all` ([scratchpad/AE_m1on2/xbsys1.out](../../scratchpad/AE_m1on2/xbsys1.out), [scratchpad/AE_m1on2/xbsys2.out](../../scratchpad/AE_m1on2/xbsys2.out), [scratchpad/AE_m1on2/xbsys3.out](../../scratchpad/AE_m1on2/xbsys3.out)); triage [scratchpad/m1on2-triage-disk.md](../../scratchpad/m1on2-triage-disk.md). Each rule's check was run on each machine's capture in the reference's place.

| rule | HB-F900 | NMS 8250 | FS-A1WSX |
|---|---|---|---|
| R-BS-DI | interrupts ON after every call [M] | interrupts ON after every call [M] | interrupts ON after every call [M] |
| R-BS-SLOT | IY $0314 after every call [M] | IY $0314 [M] | IY $0314 [M] |
| every other rule | holds [M] | holds [M] | holds [M] |

With a clock chip: GDATE after start-up answers the clock's date and A its weekday (not 1984-01-01, A 0); GTIME answers the time of day, and after STIME 12:34:56 reads 12:34:56 back; GDATE leaves F $44 and BC the clock's hour and minute, STIME E 6 -- on all three [M]. The rules this breaks: R-BS-DATE, R-BS-TIME, R-BS-WORK (its GDATE / GTIME / STIME cases only).

**[A]** R-BS-SLOT hard-codes IY per interface; it needs writing against DRVSLT: on all three IY's high byte $03 is DRVSLT's primary slot ($8B on the HB-F900 and FS-A1WSX, $8F on the NMS 8250); DE = IX after DSKRST holds.


**On the turbo R** (FS-A1GT and FS-A1ST, which agree; measured 2026-11-23, D-TRSPEC: [AE_trall/xbsys1.out](../../scratchpad/AE_trall/xbsys1.out), [AE_trall/xbsys2.out](../../scratchpad/AE_trall/xbsys2.out), [AE_trall/xbsys3.out](../../scratchpad/AE_trall/xbsys3.out); this page's rules run on that capture by [trnew_recheck.py](../../scratchpad/trnew_recheck.py): [AE_trnew_recheck.out](../../scratchpad/AE_trnew_recheck.out); overview: [turbor/remeasured.md](../turbor/remeasured.md)):

- Every rule as in the FS-A1WSX's column above, on both models (interrupts on after every call, IY $0314, GDATE / GTIME / STIME from the clock chip) [M], except one case: **DSKRST after SELDSK 1 (XS2 14) returns with interrupts OFF on the FS-A1GT**, ON on the FS-A1ST and the A1WSX; with drive A current (XS 12) all return with them on [M]. Reproduced in three runs ([AE_trall/xbsys2.out](../../scratchpad/AE_trall/xbsys2.out), [rerun/xbsys2.out](../../scratchpad/AE_trall/rerun/xbsys2.out), [rerun/xbsys2_tr4.out](../../scratchpad/AE_trall/rerun/xbsys2_tr4.out)); openMSX from an empty persistent state is deterministic, so the repeats confirm the reading, not a cause. **Narrowed down 2026-11-23 (tr6) to a timing coincidence of that one run, not a rule** (all decoded in [AE_xtrdsk_decoded.out](../../scratchpad/AE_xtrdsk_decoded.out) by [xtrdsk_decode.py](../../scratchpad/xtrdsk_decode.py)):
  - The DI is real at the return [M]: [xtrdsk2e](../../probes/bios/spec/xtrdsk2e.asm) is xbsys2 byte for byte (same ROM) with the debugger reading the CPU's interrupt flip-flops at the instruction after CALL BDOS (biosl_run's `@iffsnap`; a breakpoint takes no emulated time, no LD A,I): case 14 IFF = 0 on the A1GT, $07 on the A1ST (`reg IFF`: interrupts on); every other case $07 on both ([AE_xtrdsk2e_tr.out](../../scratchpad/AE_xtrdsk2e_tr.out)).
  - Nothing that moves the code reproduces it [M]: the same 17 calls in another frame ([xtrdsk2a](../../probes/bios/spec/xtrdsk2a.asm), IX / IY as xbsys2; [xtrdsk2c](../../probes/bios/spec/xtrdsk2c.asm), IX = IY = 0; [xtrdsk2b](../../probes/bios/spec/xtrdsk2b.asm), without the SDATE / GDATE / STIME cases) and xbsys2 with one LD A,I added right after the call ([xtrdsk2d](../../probes/bios/spec/xtrdsk2d.asm)) all return EI on both models. [xtrdskrst](../../probes/bios/spec/xtrdskrst.asm)'s 16 groups -- DSKRST as the first call; after SELDSK 1, SELDSK 0, SELDSK 2 (no drive C), SELDSK 1 + 0; repeated; entered EI; 120 frames after SELDSK; CURDRV between; right after ALLOC E 0 / E 1; on the R800 in ROM and in DRAM mode -- all return EI on both, A $00, F $42 ($62 on the R800), BC $9F0D, HL $9F00, DE = IX = $F195 / $F19A (the work-area offset below), drive A current afterwards. A frame-phase sweep -- each trial EI / HALT / DI, then k x 26 T-states, SELDSK 1, DSKRST, k = 0..2399, a little over one frame -- never returns DI on either model, with or without an ALLOC E 1 just before each trial ([xtrdsk4a](../../probes/bios/spec/xtrdsk4a.asm), [xtrdsk4b](../../probes/bios/spec/xtrdsk4b.asm); 0 of 2400 each).
  - So it is neither drive B, the order of the calls, the entry IX / IY, the CPU mode nor the model as such [M]; [P] some phase in xbsys2's run on the A1GT that the sweeps did not lock -- the disk's rotation (never synchronised by any probe here) is the untested candidate. A program cannot rely on the interrupt state DSKRST leaves on the turbo R: EI after it if interrupts are needed [P].
  - Drive B "present" could not be varied: both openMSX turbo R machine configurations have ONE drive on the built-in FDC, so drive B is always the phantom drive; a second drive needs a modified machine configuration (not done).
  - The Z80's LD A,I quirk is emulated on the turbo R [M]: with interrupts on, 0 to 2 of 4096 back-to-back LD A,I read P/V = 0 ([xtrdsk3a](../../probes/bios/spec/xtrdsk3a.asm)'s OP $F2 traces) -- why these probes read the flag three times, or through the debugger. The A1GT's disk work area lies 5 bytes lower than the A1ST's / A1WSX's (IX after DSKRST $F195 vs $F19A, after ALLOC $F180 vs $F185) [M].
