# `CALBAS` ($0159) — call a routine in the main ROM

> **Spec:** FINAL (2026-11-18), after the second reviews (two): round 4 measured what they found unseparated; the
> rest is open. Measured on the VG-8020, alone and with a disk interface, and on
> the CF-3300, with the published RSLREG as the target.

## Summary

`CALBAS` calls the routine at IX in the main ROM and returns its results. Measured only with
RSLREG ($0138), in page 0, which is the main ROM before the call: nothing had to be switched. Its A
(the slot register during the call), and F, BC, DE, HL come back. IX is $0138 after (whose, the call's
or RSLREG's, is open) and IY $0000. After the call the slot register is as before it, and interrupts are off.

zerobas does all of the measured behaviour the same.

## The specification

References: the Philips VG-8020's main BIOS, alone (row `N`) and with an NMS 1200 disk interface
(row `D`), and the National CF-3300's (row `C`), whose RAM is the expanded slot 3-0 as zerobas's is.
Ours: the C-BIOS repack, diskless on row `N`, the disk build on `D` and `C`.

**Evidence.** **[XC rNN]**: case `NN` of the XCALSLT probe, run 4, on row `r`:
[`scratchpad/XCALSLT_run4.out`](../../scratchpad/XCALSLT_run4.out) (`N`),
[`scratchpad/XCALSLT_run4d.out`](../../scratchpad/XCALSLT_run4d.out) (`D`),
[`scratchpad/XCALSLT_run4c.out`](../../scratchpad/XCALSLT_run4c.out) (`C`).
`probes/bios/spec/xcalslt.asm` documents the cases and the 40-byte record:
- The probe runs from RAM in page 3. Its target is its own routine at $4010, run in the cartridge's
  slot (page 1, or page 2 where the 16 KB image is mirrored) or, copied there by hand, in the RAM
  slot's page 1. Before each call page 1 is set by hand to another slot than the target's.
- In the target the routine records the slot register, the interrupt state, slot 3's secondary
  selection, the stack depth below the caller's SP and every register it was handed, then returns
  AF $C3D7, BC $1122, DE $3344, HL $5566.
- The target also sets IX $A55A and IY $5AA5 before returning, so what comes back shows whose
  values they are.
- Inputs: A $A5, F $00, $5A or $FF; CALSLT's IY low byte $65 or $C3; interrupts on or off.
- Cases 00, 01, 04, 06, 10, 12, 15, 17, 19 are CALSLT; 02, 03, 05, 07, 11, 13, 16, 18 CALLF; 08 and 09 CALBAS (its target
  is RSLREG, $0138, in page 0, which is the main ROM before the call). Case 14 is a CONTROL: the
  target called directly with interrupts on, to show the readout reading them on.
- RAM targets are in page 1 (06, 07, 10, 11, 19) and page 2 (12, 13, 17, 18). Where slot 3 is
  expanded, cases 10, 11 and 15 first set page 1's secondary selection to 3-1 by hand, and 16, 17,
  18 page 2's. Cases 15 and 16 are cartridge targets under such a preset; 15 also has IY low $C3.
- The RAM is slot 3 on the VG-8020 and the expanded 3-0 on the CF-3300 and zerobas's machine: on rows
  `N` and `D` the RAM cases' stack depth, IY in the target and secondary selection come from
  different paths (and the secondary preset runs on zerobas only), so they are not compared there:
  those cases are a group without those outputs (the reference's own values -- a depth of 6, IY the
  slot over the caller's low byte -- are not counted).

## Differences from the reference

None measured: every rule holds on zerobas.

## Open (not measured)

- **[A]** A target in page 1 or 2 of the main ROM (BASIC, CALBAS's real use), where the call must
  switch a page: its routines are not published, and the clean room reads none of them; any routine
  but RSLREG.
- **[A]** Whose IX comes back: RSLREG does not change IX, so "CALBAS restores IX" and "the target
  left it" read alike.
- **[A]** The interrupt state, the stack depth and the registers inside the target (RSLREG records
  none of them); a main ROM in a slot other than 0, or expanded (IY's $0000).
- **[A]** A slot register other than $FC before the call (one value in all six cases).
- **[A]** The alternate registers; RAM; the time it takes.
