# `ISFLIO` ($014A) — is the output going to a file?

> **Spec:** FINAL (2026-11-18), after one review (§7's small-call stop). Measured on the VG-8020, alone and with a disk interface, at
> cartridge time.

## Summary

`ISFLIO` tells whether BASIC's output is going to a file: A is PTRFIL ($F864)'s two bytes ORed --
non-zero exactly when PTRFIL is -- and F that OR's flags (Z set: no file). The other registers are
kept.

zerobas looks at PTRFIL's low byte only: a PTRFIL of $0100 or $8000 reads as "no file" (below).

## The specification

References: the Philips VG-8020's main BIOS, alone (row `N`) and with an NMS 1200 disk interface
(row `D`). Ours: the C-BIOS repack, diskless on row `N`, the disk build on `D`.

**Evidence.** The XOUTDO probe, run 2:
[`scratchpad/XOUTDO_run2.out`](../../scratchpad/XOUTDO_run2.out) (`N`),
[`scratchpad/XOUTDO_run2d.out`](../../scratchpad/XOUTDO_run2d.out) (`D`).
`probes/bios/spec/xoutdo.asm` documents its five sections and the record; it runs at cartridge time,
before BASIC, with the emulator's printer logger plugged into the printer port.
- **[XI rN]**: ISFLIO with PTRFIL ($F864) := $0000, $0001, $0100, $8000, $FFFF (put back to 0
  after); entry A $5A, F $00 / $FF alternately.
- **[XS r0]** / **[XS r1]**: OUTDO with PRTFLG ($F416) 0 and the cursor set by hand: '~' at CSRY 5
  / CSRX 3 with entry F $00, then '|' at 12 / 10 with F $FF; the cursor before and after. **[XV r]**:
  RG2SAV and where the two are, the name table (RG2SAV * $400) read through the VDP's ports.
- **[XO rN]** / **[XP r]**: OUTDO with PRTFLG 1, NTMSXP 0: `50 09 5A 01 41 0D 0A` to the printer
  (entry F $00 / $FF alternately), then PRTFLG $FF: 'K'; LPTPOS after each, and what the printer
  received.
- Interrupts off; BC, DE, HL, IX and IY carry markers.
