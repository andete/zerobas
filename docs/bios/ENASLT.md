# `ENASLT` ($0024) — switch a page to a slot

> **Spec:** FINAL (2026-11-18): the second reviews (two) found no unpinned core rule; their text items fixed, the
> probe's B-E seeded (round 5); the rest is open. Measured on the VG-8020, alone and with a disk interface, and on
> the CF-3300: pages 1 and 2 switched to the RAM slot and to the probe's own cartridge; and, on the
> CF-3300, page 1 switched between the sub-slots 3-0, 3-1 and 3-2.

## Summary

`ENASLT` switches the page that holds the address in H (measured for pages 1 and 2; H's other
bits do not count) to the slot that A names, and leaves it switched. A is a slot ID: bits 0-1 the primary slot, bits 2-3 the
secondary, bit 7 set when the slot is expanded. The page's two bits of the slot register take the
slot's primary number. For the expanded slot 3 (measured for page 1), the page's field of the
secondary register takes the ID's secondary number, the other fields keep the register's, and
SLTTBL+3 is set to that same byte. The SLTTBL entry of a slot that is not expanded is left alone.

It returns with interrupts off, the new slot register in A and F a logical result's on it. B and C
hold the slot's primary number in the page's bits and the complement of the page's mask, D the primary number in all four pages' bits,
E the page's mask. HL, IX and IY are kept.

zerobas does all of the measured behaviour the same.

## The specification

References: the Philips VG-8020's main BIOS, alone (row `N`) and with an NMS 1200 disk interface
(row `D`), and the National CF-3300's (row `C`), whose RAM is the expanded slot 3-0 as zerobas's is.
Ours: the C-BIOS repack, diskless on row `N`, the disk build on `D` and `C`.

**Evidence.** **[XE rN]**: case `N` of the XENASLT probe, run 4, on row `r`:
[`scratchpad/XENASLT_run4.out`](../../scratchpad/XENASLT_run4.out) (`N`),
[`scratchpad/XENASLT_run4d.out`](../../scratchpad/XENASLT_run4d.out) (`D`),
[`scratchpad/XENASLT_run4c.out`](../../scratchpad/XENASLT_run4c.out) (`C`).
`probes/bios/spec/xenaslt.asm` documents the header, the cases and the 16-byte record:
- The probe runs from RAM in page 3. It works out the RAM's slot ID and the cartridge's (page 1's
  slot at boot, not expanded on any machine), and writes markers into the RAM slot by hand ($5A /
  $A5 at $4000, $C6 / $6C at $8002).
- Each case puts the boot state back by hand (the slot register, slot 3's secondary register, SLTTBL),
  puts junk into the SLTTBL entry of every slot that is not expanded ($1B + $11 per slot number),
  calls ENASLT, and reads what the page now shows, without switching anything.
- The RAM's slot ID is $03 on the VG-8020 and $83 (3-0) on zerobas's machine and the CF-3300: on
  rows `N` and `D` the two machines are handed different IDs for the RAM cases.
- Page 1 goes to RAM (from the cartridge) and to the cartridge (already there); page 2 to the
  cartridge (from RAM), to RAM (already there), and to RAM from the cartridge (set by hand first). H is $40, $5A, $7F, $80, $9C, $A5 or $BF; entry F $00 or
  $FF; interrupts on or off. B, C, D, E, IX and IY carry markers, and L is $33.
- The slot register's boot value is $F4 on rows `N` and `C`, $F8 on row `D`'s reference (its
  cartridge in slot 2, and its slot 1 expanded). SLTTBL at boot differs between the machines on rows
  `N` and `D`: set-up, which each case overwrites with junk for the slots that are not expanded.

**[XE2 n]**: case `n` of the XENASLT2 probe, run 5, on row `C` only,
[`scratchpad/XENASLT2_run5c.out`](../../scratchpad/XENASLT2_run5c.out)
(`probes/bios/spec/xenaslt2.asm`). Under Joost's ruling of 2026-11-18 it reads 3-1 only at its
header ($4000 / $4001, "AB"). It sets slot 3's secondary register and SLTTBL+3 by hand for pages 0-2
(page 1 to 3-0, 3-1 or 3-2; pages 0 and 2 to other sub-slots in some cases; the two set to different
patterns in two cases), then calls ENASLT naming 3-0 or 3-1 for page 1, once with interrupts on.

## Differences from the reference

None measured: every rule holds on zerobas.

## Open (not measured)

- **[A]** Pages 0 and 3 (the BIOS and the probe's code live there); page 2 to a sub-slot; the
  secondary register's page-3 field other than 3-0.
- **[A]** L other than $33 (only H's bits 6-7 counting is measured against H's other bits).
- **[A]** HL, IX, IY and the SLTTBL entries other than +3 on the secondary-slot path (XENASLT2
  seeds B-E only); the page-0 / page-3 fields of the slot register with values other than 0 / 3.
- **[A]** Sub-slots other than 3-0 / 3-1 as the target, an expanded slot that is not page 3's own,
  and bit 7 against the slot's kind.
- **[A]** Bits 4-6 of the slot ID.
- **[A]** A third slot-register value; IX / IY with a second set of markers.
- **[A]** The alternate registers and SP; other RAM; the time it takes.
