sub  fmt_menu_text  Deliberately kept at its ORIGINAL pre-eviction position between fmt_boot and fmt_geom_byte to preserve the byte layout of basic/format-body.inc (its own comment says so). The menu itself stays main-resident (docs/spec-evict-call-format.md §2), so the sub-side copy is unreachable by design. 16 B of a ROM with 7411 B free — worth more as this gate's vacuity canary than as recovered bytes.
sub fat_io_open D-PROLOGUE (fix 7) made this reportable for the first time: basic/fatio-body.inc is shared BYTE-IDENTICALLY between the resident main ROM and the sub-ROM BLOAD tenant, and on the SUB side the tenant enters at fat_io_find, never at fat_io_open — D-BLNF's whole point is telling NOT FOUND from a mount/I-O fault without the mount (see this body's own header). So these 4 B (`call fat_mount` + `ret c`) are dead in sub and load-bearing in main. Fix (4)'s remedy is `IF SUB_BUILD`, and it is DECLINED here with a reason: byte-identical sharing is this file's stated design property ("the identical source text becomes a cross-slot round trip on one side and four plain in-page calls on the other"), and 4 B of a page with 1624 B free (2026-08-22) does not buy breaking it. Allowlisted rather than gated, on the fmt_menu_text precedent directly above.
disk fat_io_open D-REARMSENS/step 9 (spec-diskcode-eviction.md 6.6af): the H_FOPEN row is now INSTALLED and 15 of the 16 spans this file used to allowlist for D-DPLMOVE are live -- the canary worked, and those lines are deleted. This ONE span is still dead, and for the SAME reason as the `sub fat_io_open` entry directly above, not for the retracted 6.6p one: hk_dpload calls fat_mount and fat_io_find SEPARATELY, because their two carries are what tell `file not found` from a mount/I-O fault (D-BLNF), which is the whole reason fatio-body.inc carries the zero-byte fat_io_find label. fat_io_open is the combined entry and only main uses it. Same remedy declined for the same reason: byte-identical sharing of fatio-body.inc across three builds is the file's stated design property, and 4 B does not buy breaking it with `IF`. DELETE THIS LINE if a disk-side caller ever enters at fat_io_open.
