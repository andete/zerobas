lineerr: the two SLOW_ROWS carry their 12 s on run_gap, not on step

The last site in the tree still paying for the conflation `run_gap` was added
to end. `step` is two knobs wearing one name -- the inter-line KEYBUF spacing
that guarantees the previous chunk was consumed, and the RUN..capture
completion budget. `c.32767` and `c.m32768` genuinely need the second: zerobas
rasterises over the true int16 endpoints and masks per pixel, so a 32767-pixel
span really is walked pixel by pixel where the references clip first and walk
~250. Raising `step` bought that window and charged it to all twelve typing
slots as well.

Every word of the measurement in that comment is about what happens AFTER RUN,
which is exactly and only what `run_gap` covers.

THE WINDOW IS UNCHANGED, AND THAT WAS CHECKED BEFORE BOOTING ANYTHING.
scratchpad/rungap/check_timeline.py reads `_tcl`'s own generated timeline with
no emulator: RUN..capture is 12.0 s in both configurations, on all three sides,
and it carries a control showing a row with no SLOW_ROWS entry is untouched.
The per-case schedule ends at 166.5 -> 52.5 emulated s (vg8020/zb) and
196.5 -> 91.5 (cf3300).

VERIFIED THE ONLY WAY THAT HAS TEETH: 236 report rows byte-identical across the
8 shards, against a baseline that two independent runs at HEAD had already
agreed on row for row. The two subject rows read ' 0 , 32767 , 21 ' and
' 0 , 32768 , 21 ' on all three machines, before and after.

AND THE TALLY WITNESSED THE CHANGE. The two shards holding these rows had a max
signal instant of 182.7 / 182.6 emulated s; it is now 78.0, the same as an
ordinary row. 0 fallbacks before and after -- which proves the 12 s is still
SUFFICIENT, not that it is still needed. With capture-on-signal that distinction
stopped mattering: the budget only costs time on a row that fails to signal.

Six case-runs, -666 emulated s, about 2 s of wall. Filed as bookkeeping and it
stayed bookkeeping; no wall claim is made. The battery came in at 540 s against
672 s for the previous full run at the same 14 s warm-up, and 2 s cannot be
132 s -- a 14 s serial warm-up does not bound contention over a 540 s parallel
battery.

make gates: 37/37 green (lineerr#1/8 red once on contention, green on the
serial retry). ROM hashes 41b8c4ed ae796ccb 7d27b871, unchanged.
