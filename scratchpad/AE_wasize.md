# D-DISKROMHOST step 4 (d): SIZING the base-addressed work area

The SIZE step that Joost ruled on 2026-11-26 in [todo/open/D-DISKROMHOST.md](../todo/open/D-DISKROMHOST.md). Nothing was
built and no tracked file changed. Symbol values come from [build/disk.sym](../build/disk.sym), dated 2026-11-26 18:19.
That build is current: it is newer than every `disk/*` source, and the 18:35 HEAD commit touched no disk source after it.
The probe and count scripts were kept out of the repo in the session scratchpad. Their raw outputs are the
`scratchpad/AE_wasize_*.out` files cited below.

## Summary

* **The ~2.5 KB was the right order of magnitude for the FULL rewrite, but the full rewrite is not needed.**
  * The disk ROM has **882 sites** that name RAM in `$C000..$F37F`.
  * **789** of them name zerobas-private cells below `$F1C9`, which a full base-addressed scheme would have to rewrite:

    | cells | sites |
    |---|---|
    | disk-only cells in the H.RUNC claim | 516 |
    | cells shared with main | 212 |
    | the INIT-private block | 11 |
    | the DOS bridge block | 50 |

  * As scheme B (the base word plus an inline-offset helper), they cost **+2.6 .. +3.6 KB**.
  * As scheme A (an IY window on the hot FAT/BDOS block, B for the rest), they cost **~+2.3 .. +2.6 KB**. That includes ~180-370 B
    of IY reloads at ~46 entries.
  * Either one is 4-6 times the 583 B free.
* **What the references do (measured, both of them):** a foreign claim of N bytes moves everything they keep **below `$F1C9`**
  down by exactly N. That covers SLTWRK's base, the DPB, the three sector buffers, the `$4030` work-area pointer (and with it
  MSX-DOS's `$0006`), the HIMEM at every moment, and FRE. **Everything in `$F1C9..$F37F` stays where it is, and they write it
  over the foreign claim.** The disk ROM wrote 16 of 16, 32 of 32, 300 of 300 and 439 of 439 claimed bytes. With 440..473 B
  claimed, it wrote exactly the 439 B of `$F1C9-$F37F` and left the part below `$F1C9` alone.
* **The CF-3300's working envelope is N ≤ 473 B.**
  * Works at N = 16, 32, 300, 400, 439, 440, 460, 465, 468, 470..473.
  * At N = 474, 475, 491, 492 and 500 the machine never reaches Disk BASIC: it restarts or comes up with garbage.
  * The HBD-F1 works at 16, 32, 473, 500 and 1024, and fails at 474.
* **Overlap:** a foreign claim is always the top N bytes, `[$F380-N, $F380)`.
  * For **N ≤ 439** it can reach **only the fixed area `$F1C9-$F37F`**, which the references also overwrite. Every zerobas
    cell there is at the reference's fixed address, except BASENT_REQ (see the inventory).
  * The first zerobas-private cell in reach is **HOOK_SLOT `$F1C8` at N = 440**. Then come DRVA_DPB at 473 (its block is `$F195-$F1A7`: the id byte, then GETDPB's 18 bytes), FILES_HASPAT at
    739, the FDC / P1_BLIT / F37D_STUB block at 862..907, the BDOS/FAT state at 1474, the buffers at 1748, and the DOS bridges at
    5102.
* **Recommendation: scheme D.** Keep every cell where it is, and let only the **numbers** follow the claim:
  * the HIMEM at each of the three claim steps;
  * DRVTBL+1 / +3;
  * the `$4030` return value.

  Widen the guard from "HIMEM = `$F380`" to "N ≤ 439". **That is ~41 B of disk ROM, 2 B of RAM and no runtime cost.**
  * Publishing SLTWRK as the references do adds ~25 B, for ~66 B in all.
  * Moving HOOK_SLOT into a free fixed-area byte (an equate change, 0 B) raises the envelope to N ≤ 472, one byte short of
    the CF-3300's 473 (the DPB block's top byte `$F1A7` is next).
  * For N ≤ NMAX, the outside then matches the reference: HIMEM at INIT, H.RUNC, MSX-DOS and Disk BASIC, FRE, the layout cells,
    and `$0006`.

## Reference measurement

The harness is the session-scratchpad probe. It boots each machine, logs every HIMEM write, and at each H.RUNC entry, the first
RAM `$0100` entry (MSX-DOS) and timed snapshots it reads these cells: HIMEM, SLTWRK's non-zero entries, `$FB21-$FB28`,
`$F323` / `$F325`, `$F341-$F348`, the pointer words `$F34B-$F355`, `$F23B`, `$F23D`, `$F33B` and the layout cells. It also
logs every address written inside the foreign claim after the claim, with only the time and the writer's page@slot.

**Clean room:** no PC value is recorded, and no ROM or RAM-resident code byte is read. In `$F1C9-$F37F` only the listed data
and pointer cells were read.

**The claimers.**
* [probes/disk/claimer.asm](../probes/disk/claimer.asm), unchanged.
* "claim-once" variants of it in the session scratchpad, which claim exactly N bytes.

**🔴 THE EXISTING CLAIMER CLAIMS 32 B ON BOTH REFERENCES, NOT 16.** Its 16 KB image is mirrored into page 2, and the CF-3300
and the VG-8020 run its INIT twice: HIMEM goes `$F380` -> `$F370` -> `$F360`, both writes from page 1 = its slot
([AE_wasize_ref.out](AE_wasize_ref.out), `WR HIMEM` lines). zerobas's C-BIOS machine runs it once (`$F370`). That
explains the "`$DE57` with the claimer" in the item (`$DE77 - 32`).

## Overlap

* Every foreign claim from a cartridge scanned before ours is `[$F380-N, $F380)`. A cartridge scanned after ours claims below
  our claim and cannot collide.
* **For N = 16**, the claim is `$F370-$F37F`. The zerobas cells there are WA_JMPTAB (`$F368-$F37C`) and SYSTEM (`$F37D`), both
  P. **For N = 32 (the stock claimer)**, F365_STUB is also in range. The references write the same range: 16 / 16 and 32 / 32
  bytes, by the disk ROM at its INIT.
* **In general:** for N ≤ 439 only P cells (and BASENT_REQ) are in reach, and the reference overwrites the same range. The first
  Z cell is HOOK_SLOT at N = 440. The full N_min column is in [AE_wasize_counts.out](AE_wasize_counts.out).
* **The real failure today is not the overlap, it is the numbers.** zerobas's claim steps are absolute constants
  ([disk/kernel.asm](../disk/kernel.asm) `install_basic_hooks` / `hrunc_h` / `layout_rebuild`: INIT_HIMEM `$F195`,
  DOS_RESV_TOP `$DF93`, DISK_HIMEM `$DE77`).
  * Without the guard, a foreign claim would leave HIMEM / FRE / `$0006` N bytes off the reference's.
  * A claim of N > 491 would make zerobas **raise** HIMEM above the foreign claim, and the BASIC layout would land on it.

## Scheme costs

Disk-ROM bytes are relative to today. There are 583 B free in 12 runs, the largest 216 B at `$74CD`
(`python3 tools/check_disk_walls.py`, [tools/check_disk_walls.py](../tools/check_disk_walls.py)).

### Per-site deltas

* **B**: the base word kept in a fixed-area cell, mirrored in SLTWRK. Each site becomes `call wa_hl` / `db off`, which gives
  HL = base + off.
  * `ld a,(nn)` / `ld (nn),a`: +2. With HL live, +4.
  * `ld hl,(nn)` / `ld (nn),hl`: +5..7.
  * `ld de,(nn)` / `ld (nn),de`: +3..5.
  * `ld hl,nn`: +1. `ld de,nn`: +2..4.
  * `ld ix,nn`: +3.
  * `jp` RAM code: +2. `call` RAM code: +6.
  * A `dw` table entry: +3, plus a ~15 B fix-up loop.
  * Two helpers, ~30 B.
* **A**: IY + d. `ld a,(iy+d)` is the same size as `ld a,(nn)`, and the word forms cost +2..3. Address-of costs +4..5.
  * IY reaches only a 256 B window. A window from `$ECAD` covers 446 of the disk-only sites.
  * IY is not free: `calbak` ([disk/driver.asm](../disk/driver.asm) `calbak`) loads IY from EXPTBL-1 for CALSLT at **32
    call sites**.
  * **IX is taken.** It is the channel-record pointer, with about 240 `(ix+d)` uses and 26 `push ix`, and calbak's target.

## Recommendation

**Build D, and fold C's equate move of HOOK_SLOT into it.**

### What it takes

1. Add a `CLAIM_D` word in the INIT-private block (`$F1AB-$F1C7`).
2. [disk/kernel.asm](../disk/kernel.asm) `init_gate` (line 3882): compute N = `$F380` - HIMEM, decline if N > NMAX, store
   -N. NMAX is 439, or 472 once HOOK_SLOT ([disk/equates.inc](../disk/equates.inc) line 349) sits in a free byte of
   `$F1C9-$F37F`.
3. `layout_rebuild` (line 5922): add CLAIM_D at entry. This covers INIT_HIMEM, DOS_RESV_TOP, DISK_HIMEM and CTRL_HIMEM at
   once.
4. [disk/init.asm](../disk/init.asm):
   * the `$4030` entry (line 70) and `drvtbl_tmpl` (line 804) +1 / +3: add CLAIM_D;
   * optionally SLTWRK.
5. Gates:
   * [probes/disk/disk_probe_guard.py](../probes/disk/disk_probe_guard.py) flips to "zerobas installs with the claimer" and
     should compare HIMEM / FRE / DSKF with the CF-3300's at the same N, using a claim-once claimer: the stock one is 32 B on
     the references and 16 B on zerobas.
   * Add rows at N = 300 and 439 (works) and at the NMAX + 1 decline.
   * Read `$0006` under MSX-DOS against the CF-3300's.
   * Run the disk corpus.

### What it does not cover

* A foreign claim of N > NMAX still declines. The CF-3300 itself does not come up at 474..500; the HBD-F1 does at 500 and
  1024.
* Cells that a cartridge scanned AFTER ours could write between INIT and H.RUNC. Today's exposure is unchanged.
* `$F34D-$F355`: zerobas still does not fill these pointers. They are class 3.
* The 128 B GETWRK band: it is still not written. That is correct per the atlas.
* Main ROM's D-CARTCLAIM, which drops a cartridge's claim at zerobas BASIC's boot.
* A second disk interface: a disk ROM before ours claims ~491 B, more than NMAX.
* Host independence for claims that do not come from the top, which no MSX convention produces.

## Open questions

1. **Is N ≤ 439 (or 472) enough?** No real-cartridge claim sizes were measured. Which RAM-claiming cartridges, such as
   RS-232C or MSX-AUDIO / MSX-MUSIC BIOS, sit in a lower slot than a disk interface, and how much do they take? The CF-3300's
   own ceiling is 473 B.
2. **Why do both references fail at exactly N = 474,** that is HIMEM `$F1A6` at the disk ROM's INIT, when the HBD-F1 passes
   at 500 and 1024? It was not investigated: it is reference internals, and the clean room stops there. It could be an
   artefact of a claimer that only writes HIMEM. One way to check: a claimer that also builds the MS-BASIC layout under its
   HIMEM.
3. **Publish SLTWRK now (+25 B), or keep it as the separate "NOT DONE" it already is in the item?**
4. **BASENT_REQ `$F33D`** is our own signature in the reference's fixed area. A foreign claim of 66 B or more covers it, just
   as it covers the reference's own cells there. Keep it there, or move it?
5. **A>BASIC (`bt_keep`) with a claimer present:** zerobas resets HIMEM to `$F380` and re-runs the cartridge INITs, so the
   claimer claims again. The CF-3300 + claimer gives `$DE57` again at A>BASIC. This should be confirmed on zerobas once D is
   built.
