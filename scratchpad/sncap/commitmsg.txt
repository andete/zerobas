probe harness: six more gates capture on the program's SIGNAL

D-SNCAP2 extends capture-on-signal from basic_probe_graphics.py to every other
boot-per-case emulator gate that can take it: penderr, screenerr, stmtpend,
tmfp, lineerr and deffn-strict. 1344 captures on signal, 1 fallback, all six
green, ROM hashes unchanged.

The plumbing is no longer copy-pasted. probes/lib/probe_signal.py now owns the
ADDRESS, the kwargs and the tally -- the shape-free parts, where a second copy
is how two probes end up watching two addresses. basic_probe_graphics.py
delegates to it and keeps paint_mark(), because WHERE THE MARK GOES is a
property of the programs a probe builds and is the only part that can be
silently wrong. Its own reading is unchanged: 321 on signal, 0 fell back.

VERIFIED THE ONLY WAY THAT HAS TEETH. The tally proves a mark was never
REACHED; nothing in it would catch a mark placed too EARLY, which captures a
half-finished machine on both sides and can agree wrongly. So all 557 report
rows across 13 reports were diffed before vs after through probe_report.parse
(scratchpad/sncap/rowdiff.py, itself teeth-checked against a mutated log):
byte-identical, every one.

A THIRD CONVERSION RULE, LEARNED ON THE THIRD GATE. A mark is BASIC TEXT: it
makes the tokenised program ~8 bytes longer per marked line, VARTAB sits
directly above the program text, so a readout that IS a RAM address moves with
it. deffn's five ADDR rows report VARPTRs; marking them shifts z.addr.ctl and
o.sameaddr. Neither is gated, so this would not have gone red -- it would have
quietly moved a recorded characterization number to accommodate the instrument.
They keep the budget.

AND THE MARK IS NOT FREE EVEN WHEN THE RULES PASS. screenerr reclaims only
-5.1% against -11 to -13% elsewhere, because POKE&HE000,255 pushes its printing
line from 36 to 51 characters, past the 38-char KEYBUF chunk boundary, buying
an extra typing slot that gives half the reclaim back.

THE DETECTOR EARNED ITS KEEP ON ITS FIRST RUN, and could not say so. deffn's
o.clearwipe3 never signalled -- its CLEAR wipes the ON ERROR handler along with
the DEF FN table, so the row aborts UNTRAPPED and reaches neither END. Correct,
unchanged, and invisible to everything else in the tree. But the tally printed
"1 fell back" out of 72 and the gate had to be RE-RUN to find out which row: an
unnamed outcome reads as no outcome. Tally.add(label=...) now names them.

NO WALL CLAIM IS MADE AND NONE CAN BE. The deterministic figure, computed off
_tcl's own generated timeline with no emulator at all
(scratchpad/sncap/emutotal.py): 71104 -> 63927 emulated s, -10.1%, about 21 s
of wall at spec-probe-budget.md's flat 0.003 s/emulated s. Against that, the
emulator-free warm-up control moved 7 s -> 14 s between the before and after
batteries and two identical converted runs of the same battery differed by 59 s.
Measured signal instants land within 0.1-0.7 emulated s of the computed t_run
floor, so the budgets they replace were pure idle.

Docs: spec-probe-mark.md WRITTEN -- omsx_repl.py and spec-probe-budget.md have
both cited it since before it existed, and a third adopter made the dangling
reference load-bearing. It carries the four conversion rules, the prompt burden
with a per-readout table, the verification protocol and the adoption record.
spec-probe-budget.md section 6 said the sentinel had been "retired as a speedup"
and was "not safe for screen captures"; the first is still true and still not
the point, the second was falsified by the 2026-08-25 decision and by 557 rows.
Two stale comments in omsx_repl.py said the same and are corrected in place.
